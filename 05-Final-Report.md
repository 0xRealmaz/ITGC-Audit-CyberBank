# Final Audit Report – CyberBank ITGC Audit

## Executive Summary

This report presents the results of an IT General Controls (ITGC) audit conducted on the CyberBank Demo Environment.

The objective of this audit was to evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of key IT controls, including access management, authentication, change management, backup and recovery, and system monitoring.

---

## Overall Conclusion

The audit identified significant control deficiencies across all reviewed IT domains.

CyberBank demonstrates a **low level of IT control maturity**, with multiple critical weaknesses that increase exposure to operational and security risks.

---

## Key Findings Summary

The following major issues were identified:

- Weak access control and lack of Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Absence of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- No formal backup and recovery process
- Lack of logging and monitoring capabilities
- Weak or missing change management controls

---

## Risk Impact

These deficiencies expose CyberBank to:

- Unauthorized access to sensitive systems
- Data loss due to lack of backups
- Undetected security incidents
- Uncontrolled system changes
- Operational disruption

---

## Recommendations Summary

It is recommended that CyberBank:

- Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Deploy centralized logging and SIEM solution
- Establish automated backup and disaster recovery processes
- Implement formal change management procedures

---

## Final Opinion

Based on the audit results, the overall IT General Controls environment is assessed as:

> **Ineffective**

Immediate remediation is strongly recommended to reduce security and operational risks.
