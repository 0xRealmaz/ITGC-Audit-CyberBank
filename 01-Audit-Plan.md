# IT General Controls (ITGC) Audit Plan – CyberBank

## 1. Introduction
This audit engagement evaluates the IT General Controls (ITGC) of CyberBank Demo Environment to ensure that key IT processes are properly designed and operating effectively.

---

## 2. Audit Objective
The objective of this audit is to assess the effectiveness of IT controls related to:
- Access Management
- Change Management
- Backup and Recovery
- IT Operations and Monitoring

---

## 3. Audit Scope
The scope of this audit includes:
- User access controls and authentication mechanisms
- System change approval processes
- Backup and disaster recovery procedures
- Logging and monitoring systems

---

## 4. Audit Methodology
The audit will be performed using the following methods:
- Control testing
- Document review (simulated)
- Observation of system configuration (simulated)
- Risk-based assessment

---

## 5. Expected Deliverables
- Control Testing Results
- Identified Findings
- Risk Ratings
- Recommendations for remediation
- Final Audit Report

---

## 6. Audit Standard
This audit is aligned with IT General Controls (ITGC) best practices and industry standards.
